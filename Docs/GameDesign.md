
# Kuwait Driving Simulator
## Game Design Document

### 1. Game Overview
A realistic 3D driving simulator inspired by Kuwait.

### 2. Game Engine
Unreal Engine 5

### 3. Target Platform
iPhone (iOS)

### 4. Environment
- Kuwait City inspired streets
- Modern buildings
- Highways
- Traffic lights
- Road signs
- Parking areas
- Day and night cycle

### 5. Main Vehicle
- Sports car
- Realistic acceleration
- Steering system
- Braking system
- Suspension physics
- Engine sound

### 6. Camera System
- Third-person camera
- First-person cockpit camera
- Camera switching button

### 7. Mobile Controls
- Steering left and right
- Acceleration
- Brake
- Reverse
- Camera switch

### 8. Graphics
- High-quality 3D models
- Realistic lighting
- Shadows
- Reflections
- Realistic road materials

### 9. Future Features
- Multiple cars
- Car customization
- Fuel system
- Parking challenges
- Traffic simulation
- Weather system

### 10. Development Goals
Create an immersive driving experience on iPhone.
